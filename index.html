CRISTIANO RONALDO

The Journey of a Legend

Early Years

Born on February 5, 1985, in Funchal, Madeira, Cristiano Ronaldo showed exceptional talent from a young age. He moved to Portugal at age 12 to pursue his football dreams.

Manchester United Era

Ronaldo's breakthrough came at Manchester United (2003-2009), where he won 3 Premier League titles and the Champions League in 2008.

Real Madrid Glory

His move to Real Madrid (2009-2018) marked the peak of his career, winning 4 Champions League titles and establishing himself as the greatest goal scorer.

Juventus & Beyond

At Juventus (2018-2021) and now with Al Nassr, Ronaldo continues to showcase his incredible skills and passion for the game.
